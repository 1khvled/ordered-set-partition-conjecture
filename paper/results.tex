\subsection{Certified computational ranges}

All coefficient arithmetic is integral. Real-rootedness and interlacing are
certified with rigorous Arb balls; exact Sturm counts, discriminants, and
Newton inequalities provide independent checks. No float64 root finder is
used. The parameter \(j=2\) is omitted from the new scan as requested.

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

No failure was found. Independent compact reruns reproduced all 660 \(j=5\)
rows and pairs and all 9,075 wide-range rows and pairs. The \(j=5\) audit checked 61,182 Newton inequalities and
122,892 certified interval comparisons. The wide audit checked 72,600 Newton
inequalities and 154,000 interval comparisons. Every wide-range row received
an exact Sturm count. A total of 5,012 Sturm controls were cross-checked
against SymPy and an independent rational implementation.

Partial \(j=3,4\) scans reached \(n=525\) and \(n=600\), respectively, but
were not audited and are not claimed. Full per-row logs are regenerable from
the supplied scripts; compact hash/count certificates are retained.

Wronskian reality is not used as an interlacing criterion. Sampled Wronskians
can be nonreal even when the corresponding row pair passes the certified
interlacing inequalities.
